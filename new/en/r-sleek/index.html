What to expect

Everyone responds to treatments differently. Results depend on the number and regularity of sessions, lifestyle and individual body characteristics. The treatment is not medical care, and we do not promise a specific amount of weight or volume loss.

If you have health conditions or are unsure whether the treatment is right for you, please consult your doctor before booking and let us know.
