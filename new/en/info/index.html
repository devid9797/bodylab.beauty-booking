Information before your visit

Address, entrance, parking and cancellation

Address

Augusta Deglava iela 66, “Deglava Biroji”, 5th floor, rooms 506 and 507, Riga.

Google MapsWaze
Entrance

After 5 pm and on weekends you need to enter a code at the entrance.

Entrance code
2700#
Parking

If you come by car, there is parking right next to the building: 1 hour free.

The registration machine is on the 1st floor by the lift, or register your car in the Mobilly app (zone LDB).

Cancelling your appointment

If you cannot make it, please let us know in advance.

If an appointment is cancelled less than 24 hours before the treatment, the full price of the treatment is charged.
