How BodyLab.Beauty handles data when you book online

Who handles the data. BodyLab.Beauty, Augusta Deglava iela 66, “Deglava Biroji”, 5th floor, rooms 506 and 507, Riga, Latvia. Phone and WhatsApp: +371 20888805.

What data we collect. When you book online: first name, last name, phone number and the chosen treatment with date and time. If you provide them, also your Instagram and a comment.

Why we need it. Only to book you for a treatment, contact you about the booking (for example to remind you of the visit or tell you about a change) and keep a record of bookings.

How long we keep it. No longer than needed for these purposes.

Your rights. You can ask for access to your data, or for it to be corrected or deleted – write or call us: +371 20888805. If you think your data is handled incorrectly, you can complain to the Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija, dvi.gov.lv).
